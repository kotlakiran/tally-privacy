Privacy Policy — Tally (Haptic Habit Counter)

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Tally is an offline-first habit counter. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. In short: Tally works fully offline, requires no account, and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.

1. Data we collect

None on our servers. Tally has no backend account system. All the data you create — habit names, counts, streaks, notes, reminders, and settings — is stored only on your device in the app's local database.

2. Optional encrypted sync

Sync is off by default and entirely opt-in. If you choose to enable sync or use the export feature, your data is packaged as an end-to-end encrypted file that only you can read, and is transferred through storage you control (for example, your own cloud drive via the Android file picker). We never receive, store, or have access to the contents.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to deliver the habit reminders you set.
  • Vibration — used for haptic feedback when you tap a counter.

Tally contains no ads and no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

4. Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your data to anyone. There is no third party that receives your data because your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or sync it yourself.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual habits in the app at any time, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Tally. Because we hold no copy, uninstalling permanently deletes everything.

6. Children's privacy

Tally is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. As the app collects no personal data at all, no such data is processed.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
